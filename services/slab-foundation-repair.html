Frequently Asked Questions

What causes a slab foundation to crack or sink in Houston?

Houston's expansive clay soil shrinks in dry periods and swells when saturated, repeatedly pulling support out from under different sections of a slab. Poor original drainage, tree roots pulling moisture from beneath the slab, and plumbing leaks under the foundation are the most common contributing causes.

Can a slab be repaired without piering?

Minor slab settlement with small voids can sometimes be corrected with polyurethane foam void fill and slab leveling alone. Larger or ongoing settlement typically needs piering for a permanent structural fix — our inspection determines which approach your slab actually needs.

How long does slab foundation repair take?

Most slab leveling and void-fill jobs in Jersey Village take 1 day. Jobs requiring piering typically take 1 to 3 days depending on the number of piers and access around the home.
