Foundation Piering in Jersey Village, TX

Jersey Village Foundation Repair Pros installs steel push piers and helical piers to stabilize and lift settling foundations throughout Jersey Village and the greater Houston metro. Houston's expansive clay soil expands and contracts with moisture, cyclically stressing shallow foundations — piering transfers the structure's load down to stable, load-bearing strata below the reach of that soil movement.

Foundation Piering Services We Provide

Steel Push Piers

Hydraulically driven to load-bearing strata, ideal for heavier structures needing maximum lift capacity.

Helical Piers

Screwed into the soil for tight-access areas and lighter structural loads, including additions and porches.

Elevation Surveys

Laser-level elevation mapping to document current settlement and verify post-repair lift.

Interior & Exterior Piering

Pier placement inside and outside the foundation footprint depending on access and load path.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many piers does my foundation need?

Most Jersey Village homes need between 8 and 20 piers depending on foundation size, soil conditions, and severity of settlement. Our free inspection includes an elevation survey that pinpoints exactly where support is needed, so you only pay for piers the structure actually requires.

What is the difference between push piers and helical piers?

Push piers are hydraulically driven to load-bearing strata and work well on heavier structures; helical piers are screwed into the soil and are often used where access is tight or loads are lighter. We recommend the right type based on your soil report and structure, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Will piering actually lift my foundation back to level?

In most cases piering stabilizes the foundation and recovers a meaningful portion of the settlement, though full pre-damage elevation is not always achievable or advisable. We set realistic lift expectations in writing before work begins.
