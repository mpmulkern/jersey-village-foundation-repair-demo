Frequently Asked Questions

Is a foundation crack always a serious problem?

Not always. Hairline vertical cracks under about 1/8 inch are common in Houston-area concrete and often cosmetic. Horizontal cracks, stair-step cracks in brick, cracks wider than 1/4 inch, or cracks that are actively growing are signs of structural movement and need a professional assessment.

What is the difference between epoxy and polyurethane crack injection?

Epoxy injection creates a rigid, structural bond and is used on cracks that need load-bearing strength restored. Polyurethane injection is flexible and expands to fill voids, and is typically used for stopping water infiltration through a crack rather than restoring structural strength.

Will crack repair alone fix the underlying settlement problem?

Crack injection seals and stabilizes the crack itself, but if the crack is a symptom of ongoing foundation settlement, piering or slab repair is needed to address the root cause — otherwise new cracks can form nearby. Our inspection tells you honestly which situation you're in.
