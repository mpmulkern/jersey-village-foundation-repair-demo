Frequently Asked Questions

What are signs of crawl space foundation problems?

Bouncy or sagging floors, sticking doors and windows, visible sagging beams or piers, musty odors, and standing water under the home are the most common warning signs we see in Jersey Village pier-and-beam homes.

Why does moisture matter so much in a crawl space?

Houston's humidity and heavy rainfall keep crawl spaces damp, which rots wood framing, corrodes metal piers, and encourages mold growth. Moisture control (vapor barriers, drainage correction, sometimes encapsulation) is part of a durable crawl space repair, not an optional add-on.

Can you repair a crawl space without replacing all the piers?

Usually yes. Our inspection identifies which piers and beams have actually failed or shifted; healthy support points are left alone. Most crawl space jobs replace or add support only where the survey shows a real problem.
