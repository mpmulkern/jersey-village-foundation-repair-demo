Foundation Repair Cost in Houston: 2026 Price Guide

Short answer: most Houston-area foundation repairs run $5,000 to $18,000, with minor crack and drainage work starting near $2,200 and major structural projects passing $24,500. The single biggest cost driver is how many piers your foundation needs and how deep they have to go, which only an elevation survey can establish.

Foundation repair pricing in Houston frustrates homeowners because the range is so wide, and because two contractors can look at the same house and quote figures that differ by a factor of three. This guide explains what actually sits behind those numbers so you can read a quote and know whether it is reasonable.

Typical Price Ranges by Repair Type

Crack Repair Only

$2,200 - $5,500. Structural repair of slab or masonry cracking where the elevation survey shows the home is still within tolerance.

Drainage Correction

$1,200 - $12,000. Grading and downspout work at the low end; full French drain systems around a foundation at the high end.

Root Barrier Installation

$2,200 - $8,000. Driven by trench length and depth, plus access around existing landscaping.

Pier & Beam Leveling

$2,200 - $16,000. Shimming and pad adjustment at the low end; sistered joists, beam replacement, and new pads at the high end.

Slab Piering

$8,000 - $19,000. The most common major repair in Houston. Priced per pier, so the count drives the total.

Full Structural Project

$24,500+. Extensive piering combined with drainage correction on larger or severely settled homes.

What Determines Your Pier Count

Piers are priced individually, generally $350 to $1,100 installed depending on type and depth, so the count is the number that decides your total. Contractors arrive at it from several inputs.

  • Measured differential: how far out of level the structure is, and across what distance, taken from the elevation survey
  • Where the movement is: settlement confined to one corner needs far fewer piers than movement distributed around the perimeter
  • Depth to load-bearing strata: deeper drives cost more per pier, and depth varies noticeably across the Houston metro
  • Pier type: pressed concrete piers cost less than steel piers driven to refusal, and the right choice depends on your soil profile
  • Interior support needs: some slabs need interior piers through the floor, which raises both cost and disruption
  • Access: tight side yards, pools, and mature landscaping slow the work and add labor

Why Quotes Differ So Widely

When three contractors quote $6,000, $14,000, and $27,000 on the same house, they are rarely pricing the same work. One may be proposing piers along a single wall to address visible cracking. Another may be quoting a full perimeter. A third may include drainage correction and landscape restoration the others left out entirely.

The way to compare them is to insist on documented floor elevations from each. An elevation survey is objective: it reports how far out of level the home is and where. Once you have those numbers, you can judge whether a proposed pier layout matches the measured movement or exceeds it. A contractor who will not provide the survey behind their pier count is asking you to buy on trust alone.

Costs Frequently Left Off a Quote

Ask specifically whether these are included, because their absence is a common reason a final invoice exceeds the estimate.

  • Permits, where the municipality requires them for structural work
  • Plumbing testing before and after the lift, which matters because moving a foundation can affect under-slab lines
  • Landscape and hardscape restoration where piers are installed around the perimeter
  • Interior finish repair such as drywall patching and repainting after a lift
  • Post-repair elevation survey documenting the corrected position
  • Drainage correction, which is genuinely separate work but is what stops the problem recurring

When the Cheapest Quote Is the Right One

Sometimes the low quote is correct and the expensive ones are overselling. Houston homes crack for reasons that are not always active structural movement: a decade-old stair-step mortar crack that has not moved, hairline drywall separation above a doorway, or surface cracking in a slab that measures within tolerance. If the elevation survey shows the home is level, piering will not improve anything, and drainage work plus monitoring is the honest recommendation.

The reverse is also true. A quote that is dramatically cheaper because it skips drainage entirely may fix today's settlement while leaving the cause running. Read our drainage correction page for why soil moisture is usually the actual problem.

Paying for Foundation Repair

Most Houston contractors, including us, structure larger projects with a deposit and staged payments tied to completion milestones rather than requiring the full amount up front. Financing through third-party lenders is widely available for structural work in the $8,000 to $25,000 range.

Insurance is a harder path. Standard Texas homeowners policies exclude earth movement and expansive soil damage, which is the cause of most foundation repair in this region. The exception worth investigating is damage stemming from a sudden under-slab plumbing leak, which some policies do cover. A plumbing test before assuming a claim is hopeless is inexpensive and occasionally changes the picture entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of foundation repair in Houston?

Most Houston-area residential foundation repairs fall between $5,000 and $18,000, with a median around $11,000 for slab piering on a typical single-story home. Minor crack repair and drainage-only work starts near $2,200. Large or badly settled homes needing extensive piering plus drainage correction can exceed $24,500. The determining factor is pier count and depth, not square footage.

How much does a single foundation pier cost?

Individual piers in the Houston market generally run $350 to $1,100 installed. Pressed concrete piers sit at the lower end, steel piers driven deeper to load-bearing strata at the higher end. A quote of eighteen piers at $600 each is $10,800 before drainage, permits, or landscape restoration, which is why pier count is the number to scrutinize on any estimate.

Why do foundation repair quotes vary so much between contractors?

Usually because they are not quoting the same scope. One contractor may propose twelve piers along one wall, another twenty-four around the full perimeter, and a third may recommend drainage correction with no piering at all. Without an elevation survey you cannot tell which is right. Ask every contractor for documented floor elevations, then compare quotes against the same measurements.

Does homeowners insurance cover foundation repair in Texas?

Rarely for soil movement. Standard Texas policies exclude damage from earth movement, settling, and expansive soil, which covers the large majority of foundation repairs in Houston. Coverage more often applies when the foundation damage results from a covered event such as a sudden plumbing leak under the slab. Check your specific policy language; a plumbing test is worth running before assuming a claim is impossible.

Is foundation repair worth it before selling a Houston home?

Generally yes when movement is active and documented. Unrepaired foundation issues almost always come up in a buyer's inspection and get negotiated at more than the repair cost, plus they shrink the buyer pool. A completed repair with a transferable warranty and before-and-after elevation readings converts an open-ended risk into a closed file, which is easier for a buyer to accept.

How long should foundation repair last?

Properly installed piers are a permanent structural fix and are normally warrantied for the life of the home. What fails is not usually the piers but the surrounding conditions: if drainage is left uncorrected, untouched areas of the same foundation can move later. That is why a repair addressing only the symptom often looks like it did not last, when in fact a different section moved for the same unresolved reason.
