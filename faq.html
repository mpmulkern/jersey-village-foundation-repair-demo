How much does foundation repair cost in Jersey Village, TX?

Foundation repair in the Jersey Village / Houston area typically runs $2,200–$24,500+, depending on the number of piers needed, foundation type, and severity of settlement. A single-pier crack repair sits at the low end; a full perimeter piering job on a large slab sits at the high end. What are the warning signs I need foundation repair?

Sticking doors and windows, cracks in interior walls or above door frames, uneven or sloping floors, gaps between walls and ceilings, and visible exterior cracks wider than 1/4 inch are the most common warning signs in Jersey Village and Houston-area homes.

Why does Houston have so much foundation movement?

Houston and Jersey Village sit on expansive clay soil, which shrinks significantly during dry periods and swells when saturated with rain. This constant expansion and contraction places uneven stress on shallow foundations, which is why the region sees more foundation repair activity than areas with stable, non-expansive soil.

Will foundation repair be covered by insurance?

Standard homeowners insurance generally does not cover foundation settlement from normal soil movement, since it is considered a gradual, maintenance-related issue rather than a sudden event. Coverage may apply if settlement is directly caused by a covered peril such as a plumbing leak; we can help you understand which situation applies to your case.
