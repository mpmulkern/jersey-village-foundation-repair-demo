We handle piering, slab leveling, root barrier installation, crack repair, and drainage correction, and every project opens with a documented elevation survey so the pier count comes from readings rather than guesswork.

Champions presents a specific combination we see nowhere else in our coverage area to the same degree: older slabs on high-plasticity clay, under a canopy of mature hardwoods that were already substantial when these houses were built. What We See on Champions Foundations

Root-driven clay shrinkage leads by a wide margin. The elevation signature is a smooth gradient falling toward the nearest large tree, lowest at the corner closest to the trunk and recovering steadily across the grid. Homeowners describe it seasonally: doors binding by late August, easing again after a wet winter, with a little less recovery each year until the drop stops reversing at all.

The second pattern is age-related plumbing failure. Cast iron drain lines installed in the 1960s and 1970s are now well past their service life, and a separated joint discharging beneath a slab washes fine clay out from under it. That produces a rounded dip rather than a gradient, and it is worth testing for before anyone quotes piering, because piers will not stop a leak.

Third, and less common here than in the newer suburbs, is perimeter erosion from poor lot grading. Champions lots are generally larger and better drained than the 1990s subdivisions west of here, so this tends to be a contributing factor rather than the primary cause.

Our Champions Inspection Process

  • Interior elevation survey recorded on a numbered floor grid, establishing exactly where the slab sits
  • Exterior perimeter walk documenting brick cracking, joint separation, and slab-edge exposure
  • Tree inventory with canopy spread and trunk distance measured against the foundation line
  • Perimeter moisture readings to map wet and dry zones beneath the slab edge
  • Lot grade and runoff assessment, including downspout discharge and drainage paths
  • Hydrostatic testing where the elevation pattern or the home's age points at the plumbing
  • Written report with elevation readings, diagnosis, itemized pricing, and warranty terms

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does foundation repair cost in Champions, TX?

Crack repair and minor stabilization on a Champions home generally runs $2,200 to $5,500. Slab piering typically falls between $8,500 and $19,000 depending on the pier count the elevation survey calls for and the depth needed to reach load-bearing strata. Larger projects combining piering with root barrier and drainage work can exceed $24,500. Every figure follows a documented survey of your slab rather than a square-foot estimate.

Why do so many Champions homes have foundation movement?

Two reasons that compound each other. The neighborhoods around Champions Golf Club were developed from the 1960s through the 1980s, so these slabs have been through fifty-odd seasonal cycles of clay swelling and shrinking. The area also has the heaviest mature tree canopy in northwest Harris County, and large hardwoods draw enormous volumes of moisture out of the soil beside a foundation through a dry summer.

Do the mature trees in Champions have to come down to fix the foundation?

Almost never, and we would not lead with that recommendation. A vertical root barrier installed between the tree and the slab cuts the moisture draw on the foundation side while the root system keeps feeding from the rest of its spread. Removing a mature oak also has its own risk, since the soil it kept dry then rehydrates and swells, which can move a slab upward.

Is foundation repair in Champions covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not. Standard Texas policies exclude settlement caused by soil movement, which covers most of what we repair here, including root-driven drying. The exception is a sudden covered event such as an under-slab plumbing failure washing out support. Where the evidence points that way we document it with readings and photographs during the inspection so you can take it to your carrier.

How quickly can you inspect a home in Champions?

Champions is roughly twenty-five minutes from our Jersey Village base and sits on our regular northwest route, so inspections are normally available within one to two business days. The appointment runs sixty to ninety minutes, you get the elevation readings and our diagnosis on site, and the written report with itemized pricing follows within one business day.

Warranty on Champions Foundation Work

Piering on a Champions home carries our lifetime transferable warranty and moves with the property if you sell it. Root barrier and drainage work carries five years on workmanship, and crack injection ten years on the repaired section. Those terms differ for a reason worth stating plainly: piers reach strata that does not move with the seasons, while barriers and drains sit in the soil zone that does.
