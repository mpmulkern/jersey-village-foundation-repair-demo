Foundation Repair in Houston, TX

Jersey Village Foundation Repair Pros repairs and stabilizes residential foundations across northwest Houston, working from a base inside the metro rather than dispatching across it. We handle piering, slab leveling, crack repair, void fill, and drainage correction, and every project opens with a documented elevation survey so the pier count comes from measured readings rather than an estimate off the square footage.

Houston is one of the hardest cities in the country to keep a slab level in, and the reason sits underneath every neighborhood in it. The clay beneath the metro has a high plasticity index, so it expands when it takes on water and contracts when it gives it up, and the local climate swings between both extremes within a single year. A foundation here is not resting on stable ground; it is resting on soil that changes volume with the weather.

What We See on Houston Foundations

Seasonal clay movement is the baseline condition, affecting essentially every slab-on-grade home in the city to some degree. It produces a broad tilt across the foundation, worst at the perimeter where the soil dries fastest, and it is cumulative: each dry summer takes a little that the following wet season does not fully give back.

Tree-driven drying is the most common accelerant, particularly in the older neighborhoods with mature canopy. The elevation grid shows a smooth gradient falling toward the trunk, and a root barrier often does more good than piering where the movement is still modest.

Under-slab plumbing failure is the third pattern and the one most often missed. Houston's older housing stock still carries a great deal of cast iron drain line, and a separated joint discharging beneath a slab washes fine clay out from under it. We test for it before quoting structural work, because piers driven over a running leak will not hold the repair.

How much does foundation repair cost in Houston, TX?

Crack repair and minor stabilization generally runs $2,200 to $5,500 on a Houston home. Slab piering typically falls between $8,500 and $19,000 depending on the pier count the elevation survey calls for and the depth required to reach load-bearing strata. Projects combining full perimeter piering with drainage correction can exceed $24,500. Pricing follows a documented survey rather than a square-foot rule of thumb.

Why is foundation movement so common in Houston?

The metro sits on expansive clay with a high plasticity index, meaning the soil changes volume substantially with moisture. Houston then supplies the moisture swings: long dry summers followed by heavy rainfall, plus tropical systems that saturate ground in days. A slab poured on that soil rides the swelling and shrinking beneath it, and over decades the movement accumulates rather than reversing cleanly.

Does foundation movement in Houston get worse in drought?

Yes, and the damage usually shows a few months behind the weather. Clay shrinks as it dries, so a long dry summer pulls support away from the slab edge and the cracks appear in late summer and autumn. Rainfall then swells the soil again and some movement reverses, which is why homeowners report doors that stick seasonally before the problem becomes permanent.

Is foundation repair in Houston covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not. Texas policies almost always exclude settlement caused by soil movement, which is the cause behind the large majority of repairs across the metro. The common exception is a sudden covered event such as an under-slab plumbing failure washing out support. Where the evidence points that way we document it with readings and photographs during the inspection.
